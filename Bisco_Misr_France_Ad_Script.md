# Bisco Misr × France — Funny TV Ad Script
### "Un Peu Égyptien" (A Little Egyptian)
*30-second spot · French market · Tone: warm, absurdist, self-aware*

---

## THE SCRIPT

**SCENE 1 — INT. PARISIAN CAFÉ — DAY**
A stereotypically chic Parisian man (beret, scarf in July, espresso) sits alone reading *Le Monde*. A Egyptian tourist at the next table opens a packet of Bisco Misr wafers. The crunch is cartoonishly loud — like a thunderclap. The Parisian's espresso cup rattles.

> **PARISIAN:** *(without looking up)* Monsieur... s'il vous plaît.

---

**SCENE 2 — CLOSE-UP**
The tourist holds out a wafer. The Parisian stares at it like it's a suspicious diplomatic document. He sniffs it. Once. Twice. A third time — longer.

> **PARISIAN:** *(quietly, suspicious)* C'est quoi ça?
> **TOURIST:** *(proudly)* Bisco Misr. D'Égypte.
> **PARISIAN:** *(dismissive)* L'Égypte ne fait pas de biscuits.

---

**SCENE 3 — THE BITE**
The Parisian reluctantly takes one tiny, dignified bite. Beat. His newspaper slowly lowers. His beret tilts slightly. His eyebrow goes up — one millimetre, which in French is equivalent to a standing ovation.

> **PARISIAN:** *(long pause)* ...Hm.

---

**SCENE 4 — MONTAGE (4 seconds)**
Cut to: the Parisian buying 6 packets at a corner shop. Hiding one under his coat. Eating one secretly at the Louvre behind a Monet. Texting his mother: *"Maman. J'ai trouvé quelque chose."*

---

**SCENE 5 — BACK AT THE CAFÉ — NEXT DAY**
The Parisian is now at the same table. The Egyptian tourist is gone. The Parisian has his own packet of Bisco Misr. He places it next to his espresso. Picks up *Le Monde*. Crunches — loudly, deliberately — and stares into the camera.

> **PARISIAN:** *(to camera, completely poker-faced)* C'est correct.

---

**SUPER:** `BISCO MISR — Un peu d'Égypte dans chaque bouchée.`
*(A little Egypt in every bite.)*

**VO (warm, Egyptian-accented French):** Bisco Misr. Maintenant en France.
